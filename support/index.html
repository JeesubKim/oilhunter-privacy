Frequently asked

I bought “Gems + Unlimited Coins” but it’s not showing.
Open the gem shop and tap Restore Purchase — it re-applies your purchase for free, no extra charge.

Rewarded ads aren’t loading.
Check your internet connection and try again in a moment. Ads are optional and only used to earn extra coins or gems.

Will my progress sync across devices?
Game progress is stored locally on each device. Your “Gems + Unlimited Coins” purchase can be restored on a new device via Restore Purchase.
