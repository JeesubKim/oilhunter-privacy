Privacy Policy — Oil Hunter

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Oil Hunter (the “App”), developed by Jee Sub Kim (“we”, “us”), handles information when you use the App. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to run the game, serve ads, and process optional in-app purchases.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, or account credentials. The App has no login or user account. Your game progress — coins, gems, upgrades, leaderboard scores, and settings — is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to us.

Information Collected by Third Parties

The App uses the following third-party services, which may collect data as described in their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob — serves rewarded video ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers (including the Advertising Identifier / IDFA), approximate usage data, and diagnostic information to deliver and measure ads. On iOS we show Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt; personalized ads are only used if you grant permission, otherwise non-personalized ads are served. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • RevenueCat — manages in-app purchases. It processes a randomly generated anonymous app user ID and purchase/receipt data to validate and restore purchases. It does not receive your name or payment card details. (RevenueCat Privacy Policy)
  • Apple App Store — handles the actual payment for in-app purchases. We never see or store your payment information. (Apple Privacy Policy)

How Information Is Used

  • To display and measure advertising (AdMob).
  • To process, validate, and restore optional in-app purchases (RevenueCat, Apple).
  • To keep your game progress on your device.

We do not sell your personal information.

Your Choices

  • You can allow or deny ad tracking via the iOS App Tracking Transparency prompt, and change it anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • You can reset your advertising identifier in iOS Settings.
  • Deleting the App removes all locally stored game data from your device.

Children’s Privacy

Oil Hunter is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through a third-party service in the App, please contact us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.
